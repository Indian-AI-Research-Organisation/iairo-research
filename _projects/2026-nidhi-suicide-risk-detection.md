NIDHI (Neurosymbolic Intelligent Diagnostics for Holistic Trauma-Informed Care) is the team of Shlok Shelat, Shrey Salvi, Souvik Roy, Manas Gaur, and Amit Sheth, competing in the [IEEE Big Data Cup](https://bigdataieee.org/BigData2026/cup/) — part of the IEEE International Conference on Big Data (IEEE BigData 2026).

## The Challenge

The Explainable Suicide Risk Detection challenge asks systems to assess suicide risk from Reddit posts the way a clinician would: predicting a risk level, extracting the specific post text that supports that assessment, and identifying the relevant clinical risk and protective factors — not just a label.

## Our Approach

NIDHI's system combines task-specific modeling with knowledge-guided approaches to improve both prediction performance and explainability. The team explored multiple modelling strategies for risk classification and evidence extraction, incorporating structured, task-specific knowledge into the pipeline. The final system was built to deliver strong predictive performance alongside meaningful evidence and interpretable explanations for every prediction.

## Result

**1st place out of 55 participating teams.**
